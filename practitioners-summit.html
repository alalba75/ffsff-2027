Practitioners Summit

A full day of professional development for careers that do not yet, and may never, exist.

One day only · attendance mandatory for anyone who has ever called themselves a "creative" · 2027

Venue

The Summit is held in the tin shed behind the main car park, on account of the marquee budget being redirected to the Grand Laurel's engraving costs. Seating is on whatever was already in the shed. BYO cushion. BYO opinion, you will need it.

Capacity: 40 standing, 12 sitting, 1 sitting on the mower.
Weather policy: the Summit proceeds regardless of weather. The shed's roof is a known quantity and is not our problem to solve today.

Masterclass curricula

How to squander your marketing budget

Spend the entire allocation on a launch party for a film that is not yet finished. Boost social posts exclusively to people who already follow the account. Commission a billboard in a town that does not have a cinema. Whatever remains, spend on business cards for a title that will change twice more before release.

Overseas festival entry fees: a strategic approach

Submit to every festival whose name you cannot confidently pronounce out loud. Budget for the prestige of a laurel nobody at home will recognise. Ensure the currency conversion fee on each entry exceeds the entry fee itself, on principle. Track your rejections in a spreadsheet labelled "Building the Brand."

Pointless regional initiatives

Identify a town with one pub, no accommodation, and a heritage-listed war memorial, and propose to shoot your entire feature there for the location incentive. When asked how the crew will be housed, say "camping" with total confidence. When asked if the town has agreed to this, change the subject to your director's statement.

Pitch Reels: leverage the full potential of a screen nobody will watch

A dynamic and engaging way to showcase your range, genre, and specialist skills — accents, physicality, and "screen presence," a term nobody in the shed can define but everyone will nod at. Suitable whether you already have representation, in which case this reel goes straight to the bottom of their inbox, or you do not, in which case it goes nowhere at all, only faster.

Five hours of specific coaching, calibrated to training needs nobody has actually assessed. Comprises: three one-hour sessions in the studio, which is the shed, covering filming, reading, coaching, editing, and uploading, none of which will fit inside the hour; one hour of editing and cutting together the reel, mostly spent deciding which take best hides the boom mic; and one hour of consultation, cover letter, and introductions to potential, where "potential" is undefined and the introductions are to other people who also did this masterclass.
