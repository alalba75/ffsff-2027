Entry Regulations

Part II — Conduct, Fees & Disqualification

Continued from Part I. If you arrived here directly, please assume you already agreed to everything in Part I, since we've decided that you did.
Article 11

Fee terms

The entry fee is non-refundable, non-transferable, and non-negotiable, except that it may be paid in compliments if the jury happens to be having a good week.

Article 12

Self-applause

Filmmakers who applaud their own film during the post-screening Q&A will be escorted to a reflection room. The reflection room is the parking lot.

Article 13

Networking during screenings

Any filmmaker caught networking during a screening forfeits their popcorn privileges for the remainder of the festival.

Article 14

Notification of results

Selected films will be announced by smoke signal from the festival's office. Entrants outside line of sight assume all associated risk and, frankly, all associated blame.

Article 15

Jury decisions

The jury's decision is final, unappealable, and on occasion decided by a coin that only lands on "rejected."

Article 16

Permitted vocabulary

Attendees may not describe any film as "brave," "raw," or "unflinching" more than once per conversation. Repeat offenders will be gently corrected by Article 12.

Article 17

Immediate disqualification

Disqualification is immediate and irreversible for: bribery under $500 (below our published rate card), false claims of attendance made on your film's behalf by its cat, and any drone shot that isn't at least a little bit unnecessary.

Article 18

Acceptance speeches

All winners must deliver an acceptance speech of no more than seven words, at least two of which must be false.

Article 19

Best Picture, literally

The festival reserves the right to award Best Picture to a picture — an actual, framed picture — if no suitable films are received that year.

Article 20

Amendments

These regulations may be altered, reversed, or forgotten entirely at the festival's convenience. Ignorance of them is, conveniently, the only accepted defence.
